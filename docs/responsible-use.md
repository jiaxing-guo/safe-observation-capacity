# Responsible use

This software is intended for research on imperfect-information games, statistical identification, robust decision making, and offline bot-versus-bot evaluation.

Do not use it to:

- automate real-money gambling;
- connect to or interact with online poker clients;
- evade platform safeguards or terms of service;
- collect private information without authorization; or
- deploy an adaptive agent against people without appropriate consent and oversight.

Users are responsible for complying with applicable law, institutional policy, and platform rules.
